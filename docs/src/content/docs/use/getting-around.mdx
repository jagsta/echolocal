## The dashboard

Most of the time the device shows your dashboard: Tessera pages of Home Assistant tiles, or a big
clock with the weather and your Home Assistant lights and switches. See [Dashboards](/use/dashboards/).

Swipe left and right to move between Tessera pages, or tap the tabs on the native dashboard.

## The dock

The dock sits on the right edge of the screen. It has:

- **Settings** (the gear): every option on the device
- **Apps** (the grid): every app, see [Apps](/features/apps/)
- **Your starred apps**, like Intercom, the player or the shopping list
- **Sleep** (the moon): start the screen saver now

**Settings → Display → Dock position** moves it to the left, top or bottom edge.
**Hide dock after** hides it after 5 seconds to a minute without a touch; swipe in from its edge to
bring it back.

## Volume buttons

Each press changes the main volume by one of its 30 steps; hold to keep going.
A volume panel pops up on the side of the screen and shows the level. Tap the circle at the top of
the panel to see every volume (media, alarms, the assistant and system sounds) and pick which one
the buttons change.

**Settings → Sound → Volume buttons** sets what the buttons change otherwise:

- **What's playing**, the default: the volume of what you're hearing. A ringing alarm or timer comes first, then
  the assistant or a call, then music and video, and the main volume when nothing is playing. With
  the player card, a video or a call on screen, the buttons change that volume, even while paused.
  The panel opens on the volume they change.
- **Main volume**: the main volume, always.

The panel can sit on the left or right. Change it in **Settings → Display → Volume panel
position**.

## Mute button and shutter

The mute button cuts the microphones and the camera; the camera shutter slides over the lens. See
[Privacy](/features/privacy/). Every press and hold of the volume and mute buttons is also an event
in Home Assistant, for automations.

## Tap to dismiss

Tap to close the screen saver, a message, a ringing timer or alarm, or the voice assistant.
