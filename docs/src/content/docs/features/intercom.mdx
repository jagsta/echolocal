**Intercom** lists every EchoLocal device and every [LANovo](https://www.lanovo.link) device on
your network, found automatically. Open it from the dock or the Apps screen.

<Image src={intercom} alt="The Intercom directory, listing devices on the network" class="screen" />

## Calls

Tap the phone button for audio or the camera button for video. Video needs a camera on both ends.
During a call: mute, camera off, hang up.

The Echo Dot takes audio calls: **tap the action button to answer**, tap **volume down** to
decline. It places them [by voice](#calling-by-voice). The ring shows **Ring for an incoming call**
and **Ring during a call**.

Tap a device's info button to see its model, address, version, whether it's in this home, and what
it can do: calls, video calls, broadcasts, settings sync.

## Calling by voice

With the [EchoLocal integration](/start/integration/) installed, say **"call the kitchen"** (or
"ring", "intercom") to any assistant. Between two devices with cameras, **"video call the
kitchen"** starts a video call. The name doesn't have to be exact; the closest match is called.
Turn either off with the [voice triggers](/features/voice/#voice-triggers).

Automations can do the same with the device's `call` action, for example on a device named Hallway:

```yaml
action: esphome.hallway_call
data:
  peer: kitchen
  video: false
```

## Calling from Home Assistant

With the [EchoLocal integration](/start/integration/), an Echo Show's card has a phone button: call
the Show from a dashboard in a browser or the Home Assistant app, with audio or video. Sound and
video go straight between the browser and the Show, so the browser has to be on the same network.

<Image src={call} alt="Calling an Echo Show from its card: Call and Video buttons" class="screen-wide" />

During a video call, the button beside hang-up, or a double-click on the picture, goes full screen.

The Show sees who is calling by their Home Assistant user. To have it answer some of them on its
own, set **Answer from** to **Selected** and pick them under **Users**. The list shows the people in
Home Assistant that are linked to a user, under **Settings → People**.

## Broadcasts

Say **"broadcast"** or **"announce"** and speak your message after the tone. It ends when you stop
talking, up to 30 seconds, and plays on every other device in this home. **"Broadcast dinner is
ready"** sends the words as speech instead, using the assistant's voice; this needs the
[EchoLocal integration](/start/integration/).

A device receiving one plays a chime, then the message; an Echo Show also shows who it's from.
Broadcasts don't play during a call or while an alarm rings. Turn **Receive broadcasts** off to stop
getting them.

The `broadcast` action plays an audio file instead: pass its URL as `message`.

## Home key

Devices with the same **Home key** are in one home. Calls and broadcasts only go between devices in
the same home; a device that isn't shows **Not in this home** and can't be called. Devices with no
key are all in one home together.

Set the same key on each device under **Settings → System → Home key**, or with the **Home key**
entity in Home Assistant on the Echo Dot. The key itself is never sent over the network. A device on
an older version of EchoLocal only works with devices that have no key.

## Answering

Tap to answer. Every option is under **Settings → Connected services → Intercom** and in Home
Assistant:

| Setting | | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Incoming calls** | Accept calls | On |
| **Receive broadcasts** | Play [broadcasts](#broadcasts) from other devices | On |
| **Auto-answer** | Answer automatically | Off |
| **Answer from** | Auto-answer **Everyone**, or **Selected**: the devices you pick under **Panels**, the Home Assistant users you pick under **Users**, and calls from the [web page](/use/web/#call) when **Auto-answer API calls** is on | Everyone |
| **Auto-answer with camera on** | Send video when auto-answering | Off |
| **Pause wake word during calls** | | On |
| **Video stream** | Main or sub stream (not on the Echos, which send the main stream) | Main |
| **Ring**, **Video ring**, **Ringback** | Tones, including [custom ones](/features/tones/) | Ring, Ring, Ringback |

The **Call** and **Call with** sensors in Home Assistant show the call in progress.
